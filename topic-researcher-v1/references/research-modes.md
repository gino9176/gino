# Research modes

Default to Standard when the input does not specify a mode.

Source counts are budgets, not quotas. Stop by coverage and decision value.

## Quick

Use for a narrow, low-risk, simple evergreen question or one bounded fact check.

Typical budget:

- 5-10 useful sources;
- essential Research Questions only;
- primary-source search required for critical claims;
- lightweight competitor and market-voice work only when relevant.

Quick still requires all critical gates. Escalate to Standard when complexity, risk, or contradiction appears.

## Standard

Use by default for normal industry blogs, buyer education, trend analysis, comparisons, commercial research, and mixed topics.

Typical budget:

- 15-30 useful sources;
- all critical and most important Research Questions;
- authority discovery and primary-source search;
- mandatory contradiction search for material conclusions;
- Voice of Market clustering when buyer language matters;
- SERP Top 10 scan and 3-5 relevant-page deep review when competitor coverage matters.

Target:

- critical coverage = 100%;
- important coverage >= 80%.

## Deep

Use for regulation, policy, standards, pillar content, multi-country analysis, major investment decisions, controversial claims, high-value reports, or complex markets.

Typical budget:

- 30-60 or more useful sources when necessary;
- multiple evidence chains for major claims;
- deeper methodology review;
- mandatory contradiction and counterargument work;
- jurisdiction-specific and local-language primary research;
- expanded competitor, historical, and scenario context.

Deep is complete when the evidence gates pass and additional searching has low decision value.

## Automatic escalation

Permit at most one automatic escalation per run:

- Quick to Standard when critical evidence is missing, the issue becomes regulatory or high-risk, or material contradiction appears;
- Standard may recommend Deep when the scope becomes multi-jurisdictional, methodologically complex, or materially disputed.

Record the original mode, final mode, trigger, and added budget.

Do not downgrade an explicitly requested mode without caller approval.

## Mandatory steps by mode

| Step | Quick | Standard | Deep |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research Questions | required | required | required |
| authority discovery | critical claims | required | required |
| primary-source search | required | required | required |
| definition/geography/time checks | required | required | required |
| contradiction search | risk-based | required for major claims | required |
| Voice of Market | if relevant | if relevant | normally required for buyer topics |
| competitor/SERP scan | if relevant | if relevant | required for content-gap work |
| audit trail | required | required | required |
| schema validation | required | required | required |

## Early stop

Stop before the budget ceiling when:

- critical questions are fully covered;
- important coverage meets the mode target;
- major claims have sufficient evidence;
- opposing evidence has been checked as required;
- scope and freshness checks pass;
- the remaining questions are optional or low-value.

Never pad the source library.
