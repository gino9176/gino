# Topic Generator

## Industry Knowledge Map

Generate the initial map with AI, mark it `PROPOSED`, obtain human confirmation, and reuse it. Expand it during Monthly audits.

Adapt the hierarchy to the industry. Typical top-level branches:

- Product
- Technology
- Components
- Applications
- Standards and compliance
- Buying and supplier selection
- Problems and troubleshooting
- Comparisons

Keep the Industry Knowledge Map separate from the Topic Intelligence Graph:

- Knowledge Map = what the industry should contain.
- Topic Graph = what was discovered, approved, rejected, researched, or published and how items relate.

## Evergreen discovery routes

Use all six:

1. Industry Knowledge Map gap
2. PAA, autocomplete, related-search, or durable search question
3. competitor coverage gap
4. repeated customer pain
5. B2B buyer-journey gap
6. existing-content update opportunity

Use `why_write` for Evergreen value. Add `why_now` only when current evidence creates real urgency.

## Generation sequence

`Event / Knowledge Gap -> Audience -> Buyer Stage -> Search Intent -> Angle -> Topic Concept -> Governance -> Score -> Suggested Title`

Do not generate titles before concepts.

## Controlled vocabularies

Topic type:

`NEWS`, `NEWS_ANALYSIS`, `EVERGREEN_OPPORTUNITY`, `BUYER_QUESTION`, `COMMERCIAL_OPPORTUNITY`.

Content role:

`PILLAR`, `SUPPORTING`, `TREND`, `COMMERCIAL`, `TECHNICAL`, `COMPLIANCE`, `FAQ`.

Angle:

`EXPLAIN`, `IMPACT`, `ACTION`, `BUYING`, `COMPARISON`, `COST`, `RISK`, `COMPLIANCE`, `TECHNICAL`, `QUALITY`, `APPLICATION`, `TROUBLESHOOTING`, `TREND`, `CHECKLIST`, `FAQ`.

B2B buyer stage:

`LEARN`, `UNDERSTAND`, `EVALUATE`, `SELECT`, `SPECIFY`, `RFQ`, `ORDER`, `PRODUCTION`, `AFTERSALES`.

B2B search intent:

`LEARN`, `COMPARE`, `SOLVE`, `VERIFY`, `PLAN`, `SELECT_SUPPLIER`, `SPECIFY`, `PRICE`, `COMPLIANCE`, `RFQ`.

Choose one primary role and at most one secondary role. Do not multiply one event into a separate topic for every persona.

## Generation ceilings

- Normal signal or knowledge gap: 1-2 concepts.
- Important event: 3-5 concepts.
- Major regulation or industry change: 5-8 concepts.
- Absolute event maximum: 10 concepts.
- Important Evergreen cluster: 2-4 concepts.
- Core pillar cluster: maximum 5 concepts.

These are ceilings, never quotas.

## News transformation

Except for a truly major event, do not default to a news rewrite. Transform:

`News -> Interpretation -> Buyer Impact -> Actionable Topic`

Select only the angles appropriate to the event. A regulation usually supports `IMPACT`, `ACTION`, `COMPLIANCE`, and `RISK`; a product launch may support `EXPLAIN`, `TECHNICAL`, `COMPARISON`, and `IMPACT`.

## Title policy

Generate one title after governance and scoring. Prefer:

- Problem + Audience
- Question + Decision
- Topic + Business Impact
- Topic + Practical Action
- Comparison + Decision Context

Do not default to `Ultimate Guide`, `Everything You Need to Know`, `Top 10`, `Secret`, `Game-Changing`, `Revolutionary`, `Must-Know`, or `Best`. Use them only when the concept and evidence literally justify the wording.
