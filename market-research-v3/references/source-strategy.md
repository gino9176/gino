# Source Strategy

Build a fresh source map for every product and country.

## Tier A — primary and authoritative

Use for decision-critical facts:

- government, regulator, customs, official statistics, courts, and safety databases
- standards bodies and official associations
- company filings, manuals, catalogs, product documentation, and official announcements
- original datasets and peer-reviewed or institutional research

## Tier B — specialist context

Use for discovery, context, and cross-checking:

- established industry media
- trade publications and trade shows
- specialist research institutions
- distributor and retailer publications

## Tier C — customer language and weak signals

Use for questions, vocabulary, pain, objections, and emerging patterns:

- Reddit and specialist forums
- YouTube videos and comments
- reviews, Q&A, support pages, and communities
- PAA, related searches, and autocomplete

Tier C does not prove market size, sales, legality, safety, or technical performance.

## Query matrix

Combine product and local-language synonyms with:

- use case, problem, failure, complaint, and repair
- buyer, supplier, manufacturer, distributor, wholesale, MOQ, quote, and price
- comparison, alternative, best, review, and replacement
- market size, import, export, growth, decline, forecast, and inventory
- regulation, standard, certification, tariff, recall, and safety
- trend, launch, partnership, hiring, expansion, acquisition, and bankruptcy
- forum, Reddit, YouTube, FAQ, questions, and country or city

Record the exact query that produced useful evidence.
