# Market Research Skill

## Purpose

Analyze a product and determine:

- Target markets
- Target customers
- Competitors
- Sales channels
- Market demand
- Purchase intent

## Process

1. Identify the product
2. Analyze usage scenarios
3. Analyze target customers
4. Analyze countries
5. Analyze sales channels
6. Research competitors
7. Validate actual demand

## Output

Provide:

- 10 target countries
- 10 customer types
- 10 sales channels
- 10 competitors
- Key risks
- Validation methods
