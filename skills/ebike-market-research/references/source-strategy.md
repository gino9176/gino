# Dynamic Source Strategy

Build a new source map for every product. A fixed list is only a seed.

## Source tiers

### Tier A: primary and authoritative

Use for facts that affect decisions:

- government, customs, statistics, regulators, courts, and product-safety databases
- standards bodies and official industry associations
- company filings, investor relations, press releases, catalogs, manuals, and official product pages
- original datasets and peer-reviewed research

### Tier B: specialist interpretation

Use for discovery and context:

- established industry media
- specialist research institutions
- trade-show publications
- distributor and retailer trade publications

### Tier C: demand language and weak signals

Use to learn vocabulary, questions, pain, and emerging patterns:

- Reddit and product-specific forums
- YouTube videos and comments
- reviews, Q&A, support pages, and customer communities
- LinkedIn and other professional social posts
- SERP People Also Ask, related searches, and autocomplete

Tier C does not prove market size, sales, safety, legality, or technical performance.

## Ten-family scan

For every product, scan:

1. General web search
2. Trends and seasonality
3. Industry media
4. Competitor-owned content
5. Reddit and forums
6. YouTube
7. Associations and standards
8. Regulations, policy, customs, statistics, and recalls
9. Customer questions, reviews, support, and complaints
10. SERP PAA, related searches, and autocomplete

If a family has no useful evidence, record `No useful evidence found` rather than substituting unrelated sources.

## Query matrix

Generate product synonyms, technical names, common misspellings, local-language terms, and substitute categories. Combine them with:

| Dimension | Query modifiers |
|---|---|
| Use | use case, application, who uses, where used |
| Pain | problem, failure, complaint, difficult, dangerous, repair |
| Commercial | price, cost, supplier, manufacturer, distributor, wholesale, MOQ, quote |
| Comparison | vs, alternative, best, review, comparison, replacement |
| Market | market size, sales, import, export, growth, decline, forecast |
| Trend | trends, rising, launch, new, popular, demand, shortage, inventory |
| Company | newsroom, blog, investor, partnership, hiring, factory, acquisition |
| Regulation | law, regulation, standard, certification, tariff, recall, safety |
| Community | site:reddit.com, forum, YouTube, review, FAQ, questions |
| Geography | country, city, region, local-language term |

## Source discovery procedure

1. Identify the product's official category and customs or regulatory vocabulary.
2. Find the main global and national industry associations.
3. Find specialist media for the category and target markets.
4. Find 5-20 relevant competitors, substitutes, retailers, and distributors.
5. Find official regulation, statistics, customs, standards, and safety sources.
6. Find active communities, subreddits, channels, reviewers, and question surfaces.
7. Record source name, URL, geography, language, tier, update frequency, access notes, and intended use.

## Evidence handling

Record:

- source and canonical URL
- source family and tier
- publication date and event date
- geography and language
- exact observed fact
- any inference derived from it
- paywall or access limitation
- query that found it

Prefer one original source plus one independent source for high-impact conclusions.

## Trend-data rules

Treat Google Trends or equivalent values as relative interest, not absolute search volume or sales. Compare like-for-like terms, geography, category, and time window. Note spelling, language, and category ambiguity.

## PAA and autocomplete rules

Treat PAA, related searches, and autocomplete as question and vocabulary discovery. Availability and wording can vary by geography, language, device, and time. Record the observation date.

## Product examples

For e-bikes, suitable seed sources may include European and national cycling associations, transport regulators, customs statistics, product-safety databases, specialist bicycle media, motor and battery suppliers, brands, distributors, dealers, owner forums, and technical YouTube channels. Apply the same discovery logic to any other product rather than reusing e-bike sources blindly.
