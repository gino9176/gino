# Freshness policy

Judge freshness against the claim, not the page age alone. Record retrieval date and use absolute dates.

## Default windows

Use these defaults unless the user, jurisdiction, standard, or topic requires a stricter window:

| Evidence type | Default expectation |
|---|---|
| breaking news or event status | latest primary update; normally within 7 days |
| price, availability, inventory, or current promotion | normally within 90 days |
| company role, product, policy, or operating status | normally within 12 months and verified on a current primary page |
| current market size, sales, share, or growth | latest released period; normally no older than 24 months |
| regulation or policy | current official text plus proposal/adoption/effective status as of retrieval |
| standard or certification rule | current edition and transition notices |
| technical mechanism or durable definition | may remain valid beyond 36 months if not superseded |
| historical fact | original or authoritative retrospective evidence; label historical |

These are review triggers, not automatic truth rules.

## Temporal labels

Assign:

- "current": valid for the present question;
- "historical": accurate for a named past period;
- "background": useful context but not current-state evidence;
- "superseded": replaced, corrected, repealed, or invalid for the present claim.

Never present "historical" or "superseded" evidence as current.

## News status

Use:

- "confirmed": competent primary source or strong direct evidence confirms the event;
- "developing": core event is credible but material details may change;
- "disputed": credible sources materially disagree;
- "unverified": reliable confirmation is absent.

Distinguish proposal, consultation, vote, adoption, publication, entry into force, enforcement, delay, repeal, and court challenge.

A proposal is not a ban, law, or effective requirement.

## Dates to distinguish

Preserve separately when relevant:

- event date;
- announcement date;
- publication date;
- data period;
- effective date;
- retrieval date;
- last verified date;
- revalidation due date.

## Reuse and cache

Before reusing a Claim, Evidence Unit, Definition, Data Point, or Source, check:

- definition match;
- geography match;
- topic and claim scope;
- "last_verified";
- temporal label;
- revalidation due date;
- known corrections or superseding sources.

Cached evidence never bypasses freshness review.
